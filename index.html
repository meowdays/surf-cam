PERSONAL SURF MONITOR · NORTH DEVON + SOUTH WALES
How to read the surf: Check significant wave height, swell period and swell direction together. Longer-period swell often carries more energy; lighter winds usually help keep a surface cleaner, but wind direction must be judged against each beach's exposure. Tide stage can change how a beach break works. This dashboard shows model guidance, not a guaranteed surf-quality rating.

Marine and wind forecasts: Open-Meteo Marine API and Weather API. Tide tables and beach-safety forecast: Met Office. Data attribution: Open-Meteo and its weather-model providers.

Camera setup

Four spots are kept in this order: Rest Bay, Saunton, Croyde and Woolacombe. Putsborough and Coney Beach have been removed. Press Play inside each camera player manually. Surf data stays collapsed until you expand it.

Camera playback is manual. Expand “Surf data” below any camera to view wave, swell, wind and tide links.

Camera videos are started manually using each player’s own Play button. Surf forecasts refresh every 30 minutes. Wave and wind values are model estimates for nearby grid cells, not measurements at the exact breaking peak. Sea-level model output is omitted because it is not a substitute for a local tide table. Always check local tide times, lifeguard advice and conditions before entering the water.
